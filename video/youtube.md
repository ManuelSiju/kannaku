# YouTube package

**Title:** Kanakku: ask before you sign | Meta AI Developer Hackathon

**Description:** Kanakku is a voice-first money adviser for Indian families. Show it an insurance brochure, a loan offer, a stock tip or a suspicious call. A council of AI specialists on Meta Muse Spark answers red, yellow or green, with the questions to ask. Every number is computed by code, never by the AI. Decision support, not investment advice. The demo family, brochure and share are fictional. Screen recorded on a real phone; voices are the phone's text-to-speech.
