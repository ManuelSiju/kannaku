## Title

Kanakku: A Money Adviser for Indian Families | Meta AI Developer Hackathon

## Description

Kanakku is a money adviser for Indian families. Before you pay for an insurance plan, take a loan, follow a stock tip or answer a strange call, show it to Kanakku. A council of AI specialists on Meta Muse Spark answers red, yellow or green, in one plain sentence, with the exact questions to ask.

In one year, life insurers in India received 26,667 complaints of unfair selling (IRDAI Annual Report 2024-25). Most families have no adviser to turn to at the moment a seller is sitting in front of them. Kanakku is built for that moment.

WHAT YOU SEE IN THE DEMO
- A brochure promising 15 lakh: Kanakku finds that only 7.5 lakh is written as guaranteed, and says Stop
- A council of specialists: Sankz for banking, Dani for funds and insurance, Vick for stock tips, led by Alfii
- A scam call about OTP and KYC, stopped by rules with no AI call
- A guardian view: a family member sees the red answers and leaves a note
- Family papers: a demat statement read line by line and confirmed before saving
- A stock tip on a share the family already holds
- Under the hood: facts, the vote, the privacy check and the Meta model behind each answer

HOW IT WORKS
Muse Spark reads the paper and the council argues the case. It never writes a number. Code computes every figure, and each amount must be found in the paper or the question. Safety rules overrule the vote. A family's own figures stay on screen and are spoken only when the listener is private.

WHY THIS MATTERS NEXT
Today a family's money lives in separate places: bank statements, policies, loans, demat accounts and old paper files. Each seller sees one piece, and nobody looks at the whole picture on the family's side.

Our vision is a personal AI assistant that keeps that picture in sync with the family's consent: it gathers and organises their financial data, spots what clashes, and guides them before each decision. The family stays in charge, and every number stays checkable. This is where we want to go next, and it is not built yet. The next steps are consent-based data through India's Account Aggregator system, Malayalam and Hindi speech and app text, and reaching families through co-operative banks.

Stack: Flutter, Python, FastAPI, SQLite, Meta Muse Spark (muse-spark-1.3), faster-whisper, Tesseract.
